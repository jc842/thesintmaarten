# Philipsburg Front Street Guide: Duty-Free Shopping, Boardwalk & History

Founded in 1763 by John Philips, Philipsburg is celebrated across the Caribbean for its duty-free shopping privileges, pastel gingerbread architecture, and lively seaside promenade.

## Top Highlights of the Dutch Capital

1. **Front Street Boutiques**: International luxury timepieces, fine diamonds, electronics, and Cuban cigars at zero-tax prices.
2. **The Great Bay Boardwalk**: A mile-long pedestrian boulevard lined with beach bars, open-air cafes, and panoramic views of cruise ships.
3. **Sint Maarten Museum**: Preserving pre-Columbian Arawak artifacts and colonial history.

Philipsburg effortlessly blends Caribbean warmth with Dutch maritime history.
