# Cupecoy & Mullet Bay: Golden Sandstone Cliffs and Turquoise Waters

The south-western corner of Sint Maarten boasts the island's most dramatic coastal geography, where towering amber limestone cliffs plunge directly into crystal-clear turquoise waters.

## Coastal Highlights

- **Mullet Bay Beach**: A sweeping crescent of white sand flanked by sea grape trees, famous for tranquil swimming and winter surfing swells.
- **Cupecoy Beach Caves**: Carved sea caverns offering breathtaking sunset vistas across to the neighboring island of Saba.
- **Mullet Bay Golf Course**: An 18-hole championship course designed around sea-grape framed saltwater lakes.

Cupecoy and Mullet Bay offer the ultimate Dutch Caribbean beach experience.
