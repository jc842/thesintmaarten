# Simpson Bay Lagoon: The Caribbean Capital of Superyachts & Marinas

Simpson Bay Lagoon is one of the largest enclosed inland bodies of water in the West Indies, sheltered between the Dutch and French sides of the island.

## The Epicenter of Caribbean Yachting

- **Megayacht Infrastructure**: Marinas such as Isle de Sol, Simpson Bay Marina, and Port de Plaisance accommodate superyachts over 300 feet with world-class shore power and bunkering.
- **The St. Maarten Heineken Regatta**: Annual global sailing spectacle bringing international crews for world-class racing and legendary concerts.
- **Vibrant Waterfront Dining**: From local lobster shacks to chic Mediterranean bistros along Welfare Road.

Simpson Bay represents the dynamic international pulse of Sint Maarten.
